Motion Principles
Seal stamp: when a certificate is issued, the seal briefly presses down and settles — the one signature moment on the whole platform.
Status change: a status pill fades from neutral to its new color with a short checkmark draw, no bounce.
Trial ring: fills calmly as days pass — always gold, never red. This is an opportunity clock, not a warning.
Hire opportunity confirmed: the sage badge appears with a single soft pulse, then stays completely still — it should feel earned, not decorative.
Rule: nothing loops or repeats. Every animation settles within ~250ms — this is a credentials platform, not a game.
